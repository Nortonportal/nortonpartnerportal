How to Access Norton Partner Portal Login
=========================================

Follow these general steps:

1. Open your web browser.
2. Visit the Norton Partner Portal sign-in page.
3. Enter your registered partner email address.
4. Enter your password.
5. Complete any required verification.
6. Select the login option.

After successful authentication, you can access available partner portal features.

How to Reset Norton Partner Portal Password
===========================================

If you cannot access your account:

1. Open the partner portal login page.
2. Select the password recovery option.
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Follow the password reset instructions.
5. Create a new password.
6. Sign in again.
